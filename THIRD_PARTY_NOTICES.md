# Third-Party Notices

## Anvil (AhmedFathy2001/anvil-plugin)

Two things are adapted from Anvil:

- **Collection Log tab injection technique** — `BingoClogTabController.java`
  and `BingoClogIds.java` adapt the approach (and the specific measured
  widget geometry / sprite ids) used by Anvil's `ClogTabController.java` /
  `ClogIds.java` to inject a real tab into the Collection Log's native
  interface.
- **Submission banner asset and layout** — `BingoProgressBanner.java`
  adapts Anvil's `BingoClogBannerOverlay.java` (the open/hold/close
  animation timeline and text layout), and `src/main/resources/com/timeserved/bingo/clog_banner.png`
  is Anvil's `clog_banner.png` background asset, copied unmodified.

Source: https://github.com/AhmedFathy2001/anvil-plugin
